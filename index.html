How we measured it

Zas 1.15.1, the version you download here, on a PC with an AMD Ryzen 5 3600, an NVIDIA GeForce RTX 2060, 32 GB of memory and Windows 11, with a 2560×1440 screen. Test video: one minute of Big Buck Bunny (H.264, 1080p at 60 fps) and two 10 s versions made from it (H.265 in 4K at 30 fps and VP9 1080p at 30 fps).

H.264 1080p60H.265 2160p30VP9 1080p30
Open with Zas in memory378 ms134 ms125 ms
Open with Zas closed710 ms595 ms514 ms

Open: from launching zas.exe video (the same as a double-click) to the first frame on screen. Medians of 8 opens in memory and 5 from scratch. With Zas in memory, 2 of 24 opens took longer (0.7 and 2.9 s); with Zas closed, the slowest was 0.83 s. The file was already in the Windows cache.

Clip copied: marking a 12 s part and pressing Ctrl+E (no re-encoding) until the file is on the clipboard. Median of 5 runs: 0.14 s; the first took 33 s and is unexplained. Frame-accurate (re-encoding, Ctrl+Shift+E), the same part takes 4.6 to 5.0 s.

FPS while recording: Fortnite at 2560×1440 in the foreground, measured with PresentMon (Intel). With and without recording at 60 fps with the graphics card (H.264 or H.265 via NVENC), the same 61–70 FPS. Recording uses 18% of one CPU core. The old CPU method (libx264) used 2.7 cores and stuttered.

Memory: 99 MB idle (in the background, ready to open) and about 300 MB playing the 1080p60 video.

These are measurements from one specific computer: yours may differ.

Questions

What people usually ask.

Is Zas a good alternative to ShadowPlay, OBS or Medal?

Yes, if what you want is to record your screen or save gameplay moments and share them fast. Like ShadowPlay, it records with the graphics card and has instant replay, but it works on NVIDIA, AMD and Intel. Unlike OBS, there is nothing to set up. And like Medal, it has a clip editor, but everything stays on your PC: no account, no cloud and no ads. If you need to stream live, OBS is still the tool; Zas does not stream.

Does recording with Zas lower my FPS?

In our test, no. Fortnite at 1440p ran at the same 61–70 FPS with and without Zas recording on the graphics card (measured with PresentMon on an RTX 2060). Zas encodes on the graphics card (NVENC, AMF or Quick Sync), so the CPU barely works: 18% of one core.

How do I share a clip on Discord?

Save the moment with Win+Shift+G (instant replay), or mark a part with I and O and press Ctrl+E. The clip is copied to your clipboard: in Discord, press Ctrl+V. Note that Discord allows files up to 20 MB on free accounts.

What is Zas Player?

Zas (or Zas Player) is a free screen recorder, clip editor and video player for Windows 10 and 11. It opens videos instantly and has a trimmer and a photo viewer built in.

How do I download Zas?

Click “Download Zas” on this page: it downloads the official installer, Zas-Setup.exe, from GitHub. Open it, it installs in seconds and from then on it updates itself when you say so.

Is it free? Does it have ads?

It’s free, with no ads and no account to create.

What formats does it open?

Almost any video (MP4, MKV, WebM, MOV, AVI, WMV, TS…) thanks to mpv and FFmpeg, and photos in JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC, AVIF and more.
